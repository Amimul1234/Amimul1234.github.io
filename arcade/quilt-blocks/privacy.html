Quilt Blocks — Privacy Policy

Effective 25 September 2026 · Developer: The Elite Force

Summary

Quilt Blocks has no accounts and no sign-in, and it never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. The game is free and is paid for by ads from Google AdMob. AdMob collects some data, as described below.

What stays on your phone

Your best score, the game in progress, and your sound and vibration settings are saved on your device only. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner, full-screen and optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • approximate location, taken from your IP address
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data

This data goes to Google and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Menu → Ad privacy choices. On any Android phone you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Settings → Google → Ads.

Children

Quilt Blocks is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data deletion

We hold no data about you. To remove the game's local data, uninstall the app. To manage data Google holds, visit your Google account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
